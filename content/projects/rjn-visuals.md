A portfolio site for RJN Visuals, a photography and videography studio. It presents branded video work, wedding films and social campaigns, and turns visitors into booking inquiries.

## Problem
The studio needed one place to show its films and campaigns to new clients: a site that stays fast while full of video, reads well on a phone, and makes it easy to book.

## What I built
- Case-study pages for each kind of work, with YouTube films and Instagram reels embedded
- A wedding section with its own copy, films and a Book Now call to action
- A contact form that emails the studio directly, with no backend to run
- Recompressed video, metadata-only video preloading, and YouTube and Instagram embeds that load only when needed
- Labelled controls and alt text throughout

## Built with
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, SCSS Modules, EmailJS, Instagram embed widget, Vercel.
